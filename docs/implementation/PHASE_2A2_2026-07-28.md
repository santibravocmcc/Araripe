## Evidence and decisions

The 72 rasters total `13,408,183,319` bytes and have aggregate inventory
SHA-256
`c0ede11bb02cdcfbf67653dfee521f0e922955179c8da5b69c07ea5069cc1054`.
All share one EPSG:32724 `10,773 × 4,999` grid at 20 m. Minimum valid coverage
inside `araripe-implementation-rectangle-v1` is `99.700183%`; all scientific
range checks pass with zero violating pixels.

The no-rebuild decision is evidence-based but limited: historical GEE task IDs,
provider-native scene lists, source processing-baseline versions, query
fingerprint, and the twelve original export checksums were not retained.
Regenerating different pixels now would not restore those facts. The accepted
manifest records the limitation and requires a new version with execution-time
provenance for any future build.

The legacy time-series database contains 126 regional rows over 42 dates and 32
alert rows. It has ten regional write-day groups, 19 rows across nine dates
below 10% coverage, eleven dates whose indices disagree on coverage, and three
out-of-range EVI2 rows. More importantly, no row can identify its acquisition,
baseline, algorithm, extent, source scenes, generation, or QA decision.
Individual salvage is therefore prohibited.
