## Fixed candidate contract

The tracked `config/phase2a5_context_candidates_v1.json` registry was frozen
before case outcomes were calculated. It binds the two source files and
ATBDs, their full inventories, the accepted extent, exact crop windows,
categorical policies, class mappings, grid reconciliation, licence boundary,
strong-subset alternatives, contextual-signature alternatives, and an
all-false scientific decision state.

Collection 3 is the primary detailed context. Its fixed mapping places codes
`3, 4, 5, 6, 11, 12, 49, 50` in natural vegetation; `23, 29, 32` in other
natural cover; `9, 15, 19, 21, 24, 30, 31, 36, 75` in anthropic cover; `25,
33` in uncertain/mixed; and `0` in NoData. Unknown codes remain explicitly
unmapped. Collection 10.1 has a separately versioned comparison mapping and is
used only as a secondary agreement/disagreement signal. In particular,
Collection 3 code `33` remains uncertain/mixed while Collection 10.1 code `33`
is other natural cover under their respective ATBD evidence.

The strong-subset candidates are the current
`natural-vegetation-share-0.50-v1` rule and the stricter
`natural-vegetation-share-0.75-v1` rule, both using inclusive `>=` comparison.
They return `included`, `excluded`, or `not_assessed`; neither candidate can
delete, invalidate, relabel, or change the identity of a raw detection. No
threshold was tuned against or selected from an alert total.

The non-causal pixel rule retains dNBR, post-composite NBR, and BSI. It produces
only `fire_like`, `exposed_soil_or_clearing_like`, `mixed_or_uncertain`, or
`not_assessed`. The two fixed polygon-level alternatives are
`dominant-assessed-share-0.60-v1` and
`plurality-assessed-margin-0.15-v1`. They summarize every available Phase
2A.4 cloud-mask by composition stratum without selecting a Phase 2A.4 method.
Legacy causal labels, MapBiomas context, spectral appearance, and technical
integrity are not treated as evidence of cause.

## Fail-closed and tamper controls

The three builders create exclusive no-replace sibling locks and expose only
deep-validated staging trees. Existing or stale locks, normal targets, empty
targets, concurrently appeared targets, and broken symbolic links all fail
closed. Crop files publish with an atomic hard-link/no-clobber operation. A
handled failure removes only staging and outputs published by that builder;
it never removes or replaces an externally appeared path.

Tests cover source/checksum binding, accepted extent and exact outer windows,
native categorical crops, grid reconciliation, complete class mappings,
ambiguous and unknown codes, NoData and coverage accounting,
agreement/disagreement denominators, threshold boundaries, raw record order
and geometry preservation, non-causal measurements/proportions, both unchanged
drought bindings, blank reviews, five-family primary locking, exact blinding
balance, reviewer leakage and overlap, artifact inventories, schema and
provenance descriptors, coherently resealed manifests/checksums, ordinary and
broken-link publication races, and cleanup. Recomputed checksum inventories do
not bypass canonical schema, source, registry, or provenance-link checks.
