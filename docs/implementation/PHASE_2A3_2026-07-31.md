## Frozen source population and draw

The read-only source snapshot contains 31 `alerts_2026-*.geojson` artifacts
dated 2026-01-02 through 2026-07-26 and 369,177 provisional legacy
location-date features. The package records every artifact's logical key, byte
size, feature count, origin, SHA-256, and limitations in `sources.json`; their
aggregate inventory SHA-256 is
`0fa5779990ff7adc8c114a3c0bd1343c52084fc56e278524b86b0b489bb31b7b`.

The technical frame has 365,405 eligible units and 3,772 excluded units. Every
exclusion is recorded as `wholly_outside_accepted_extent`; cross-boundary
polygons remain whole. A missing source date is not interpreted as a zero-alert
date because the legacy archive has no accepted processing ledger.

Seed `20260731` selected 60 units, one from each deterministically selected
occupied joint cell. Repeated integer-feasibility checks establish a full
lexicographic cell selection; the minimum full SHA-256 unit rank wins within a
selected cell. The exact margins are:

- confidence: 20 high, 20 medium, and 20 low;
- polygon size: 20 each in `[1,2)`, `[2,5)`, and `>=5` ha;
- season: 30 wet November-April and 30 dry May-October;
- provisional land cover: 20 natural, 20 anthropic, and 20 other/water;
- provisional persistence: 20 first, 20 candidate, and 20 confirmed; and
- geography: 10 in each fixed north/south by west/central/east zone.

Selected cells have recorded cell probability 1 and units within them have
conditional probability `1/N_h`; units in other cells have probability 0. This
purposive exact balance has incomplete population support and cannot support a
population accuracy estimate.

## Claim and method boundary

The package contains no qualified human label and no final reviewer-usability
finding. It reports no accuracy, precision, recall, commission, or omission
estimate. Current alerts and derived series are provisional audit inputs, not
a clean canonical release. A detected-feature sample also cannot estimate
omission; Phase 5 still owns the independent known-change frame and final
candidate validation.

The cloud-mask, daily-composition, drought-adjustment, MapBiomas, and
contextual-signature method families are blank comparison slots with
`selected_or_activated=false`. No alternative was activated, canonized,
promoted, or used to delete a raw valid detection. Reviewer agreement, when
collected, measures interpretation reliability rather than scientific
accuracy. Tool integrity checks are not scientific evidence.

## Reproducibility limitations

- Source-alert bytes and local raster checksums are frozen, but the public STAC
  catalogs are mutable. The access time (`2026-07-31T13:53:00-03:00`), cutoff
  date (`2026-07-31`), selected item identifiers, recorded metadata fields and
  digests, search windows, runtime versions, generator source hashes, and exact
  generation command are frozen in the package manifest. Access time alone
  cannot replay a mutable catalog search.
- Rendered derivative checksums bind local panels, not upstream provider COG
  bytes. Signed query strings were neither recorded nor packaged.
- A physical place can appear on multiple source dates without becoming an
  accepted event. Legacy confidence, land cover, persistence, and contextual
  fields are provisional stratification values. The snapshot has no
  August-October leaf-off alert date.
- The browser-control integration was unavailable in this session. JavaScript
  syntax, embedded payload identity, offline folder isolation, import/export,
  schemas, and completion guards were verified automatically; actual
  qualified-reviewer usability testing remains pending.
- Redistribution and licensing review is required before any evidence package
  could leave the local/private audit context.
