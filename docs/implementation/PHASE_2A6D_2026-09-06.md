### Grid reconciliation, corrected to the property that matters

The national 10 m and 30 m lattices do **not** share an origin: the
Collection 3 crop sits at a fixed sub-cell phase of exactly (2, 1) primary
pixels inside the 10.1 grid. What nearest-neighbour correctness requires is
an exact 3:1 ratio, an integer primary-pixel phase, and that no primary
centre falls on a secondary boundary — with this phase every centre sits at
1/6, 3/6 or 5/6 of a cell, 1/6 away from any edge, so nearest is exact and
tie-free. `config/phase2a5_regional_context_manifest_v2.json` proves and
records exactly that, rehashes the reused Collection 3 crop against its v1
identity (reuse is permitted only after hash verification), and binds the
fresh export and the invalidation.
